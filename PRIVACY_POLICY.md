# Privacy Policy for DatStation

**Last Updated: March 2024**

This Privacy Policy describes how **DatStation** ("the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined here.

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
The App is designed to prioritize your privacy.
*   **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or location.
*   **Favorites & Collection**: Any data you create within the app, specifically your "Favorites" list and your "Have" (Collection) list, is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage in a private database file. 
*   **Profile Selection**: Your chosen profile character (Roberto or Moonshie) and personal bio preferences are stored locally on your device and are never shared or transmitted.
*   **Achievements & Quiz**: Your app status (Bronze, Silver, etc.) and your quiz progress are stored locally using encrypted local storage.

### 2. Voice Search
The App includes a voice search feature. 
*   **System Processing**: When you use the microphone icon, the App uses the standard Android Speech-to-Text service provided by your operating system (typically Google). 
*   **No Audio Recording**: Our App does not record, listen to, or store your audio. It only receives the resulting text from the system service to perform your search.

### 3. App Shortcuts and External Links
*   **Shortcuts**: The App provides shortcuts to launch other PlayStation-related applications installed on your device. We do not track which apps you launch or your activity within them.
*   **External Links**: The App provides a link to the official PlayStation Store. When you click this, you are directed to a third-party website with its own privacy policy.

### 4. Database Security
The App contains a built-in database of game titles and a specialized quiz database.
*   **Encryption**: The quiz database and user data are encrypted using SQLCipher (AES-256) to ensure that your collection and progress remain private even if the device's storage is accessed externally.
*   **Static Data**: Game title data is static and does not involve any user-specific tracking.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer.
